\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:introduction}

With the rapid changes as a result of the integration of advanced \acs{ict} \& \acs{iot} technologies into business as usual and daily activities, this has lead to large bodies of research in an effort to maximise the
potential these technologies and their insights can unlock. Likewise, the energy sector has seen rapid changes with the introduction of renewable energy sources, electric vehicles, and a drive to utilise
software based solutions which leverage embedded devices. This drive towards the smart grid forms the backdrop of the following dissertation, in particular, this research focuses on the aspect of energy grid resiliency.
This dissertation expands on the learnings from previous related research as part of a European Horizon 2020 project \cite{10.12688/openreseurope.14115.2}, to develop a novel approach to an automated grid resilience strategy in the context of the Irish energy grid.
